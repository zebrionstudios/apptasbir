Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tasbir collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Tasbir application and related services. Please read this policy carefully to understand our information practices.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably needed to provide and improve Tasbir.

2. Account Information

We may collect your email address, name, and account-related information when you create an account.

3. Passwords

Your password is securely processed and is not intentionally stored in plain text.

4. Uploaded Images

Images you upload may be stored and processed to provide Tasbir's image features.

5. Image Information

We may process basic information such as file name, file type, file size, and image dimensions.

6. Image Metadata

Uploaded images may contain metadata such as EXIF information depending on how the file was created and processed.

7. Public Images

Images you make public may be viewed or downloaded by other Tasbir users.

8. Image Visibility

Uploaded images may be publicly available on Tasbir and accessible to other users.

9. Download Activity

We may record limited information about downloads to provide the feature and prevent abuse.

10. Device Information

We may collect information such as your device type, operating system, app version, and IP address.

11. Technical Information

Technical information may be collected to identify errors, improve performance, and maintain compatibility.

12. Security Logs

We may maintain security logs to detect suspicious activity and protect Tasbir.

13. How We Use Information

We use collected information to provide, maintain, secure, and improve Tasbir.

14. Account Management

Your information is used to create, authenticate, maintain, and manage your Tasbir account.

15. Customer Support

Information you provide to support may be used to respond to your questions and resolve issues.

16. Subscriptions

We may process subscription information to provide the features included in your selected plan.

17. Payments

Payments may be processed by authorized third-party payment providers or app stores.

18. Payment Information

Tasbir does not intentionally store complete payment card details when payments are handled by third-party providers.

19. Third-Party Services

Tasbir may use trusted third-party services for hosting, storage, payments, security, analytics, and other necessary functions.

20. Information Sharing

We only share information when reasonably necessary to operate Tasbir, provide services, protect users, or comply with the law.

21. Selling Information

Tasbir does not sell your personal information to third parties.

22. Advertising

If advertising services are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the related data practices.

23. Analytics

We may use limited analytics or technical monitoring to understand performance and improve Tasbir.

24. Notifications

Tasbir may send notifications about your account, security, subscriptions, and important service updates.

25. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access and misuse.

26. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

27. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Tasbir account and associated personal information.

28. Image Deletion

You may delete uploaded images through the features provided by Tasbir.

29. Deleted Data

Some deleted information may remain temporarily in backups or records where necessary for security or legal purposes.

30. Children's Privacy

Tasbir does not knowingly collect children's personal information where such collection is prohibited by law.

31. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information.

32. Data Transfers

Your information may be processed or stored in countries where Tasbir or its service providers operate.

33. Legal Requests

We may disclose information when required by applicable law or valid legal process.

34. Account Security

You are responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and confidential.

35. User Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the necessary rights to upload and share images.

36. Third-Party Links

Tasbir may contain links or integrations to services that have their own privacy policies.

37. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal requirements change.

38. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at supporttasbir@gmail.com.

39. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective from 8 August 2026.
