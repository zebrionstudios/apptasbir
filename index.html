01 / What is TASBIR?

A simpler place for visual content.

TASBIR brings an image library experience to your phone, making it easier to browse and find images for ideas, projects and everyday creative work.

A useful image library should feel simple, accessible and focused.

Whether you are looking for a visual reference, collecting inspiration or finding an image for a project, TASBIR gives you a dedicated place to explore. Open the app, browse the available collection and use images that fit your needs, subject to each image's usage terms.
